# Example research questions

Answers are only as good as the papers in your library. Import the relevant literature first, then select a
collection or papers in the context panel to keep the scope focused.

## Stable water isotopes & meteoric water lines
- What LMWL slopes and intercepts have been reported for tropical island or coastal monsoon stations, and which
  regression methods (OLS, RMA, PWLSR) were used?
- Which processes do the papers in my library invoke to explain LMWL slopes below 8?
- How is 17O-excess interpreted alongside d-excess, and what measurement precision is required?

## Precipitation isotopes & the Indian summer monsoon
- What evidence in my library supports or questions the amount effect at event, daily and monthly timescales?
- Do the papers attribute rainfall δ18O depletion to local rainfall or to upstream/regional convection along
  back trajectories? Compare their methods and periods.
- How do monsoon low-pressure systems and depressions over the Bay of Bengal affect rainfall isotopes?
- What controls the seasonal cycle of d-excess in Bay of Bengal / Andaman Sea rainfall?

## Vapour, dew and fog
- How do high-frequency vapour isotope observations (laser spectrometers) relate to rainfall isotopes during
  convective events?
- What does the Craig–Gordon model predict for evaporation from the tropical ocean, and how have papers tested
  it against vapour d-excess?

## Isotope-enabled models
- How have IsoRSM, IsoGSM, ECHAM-wiso, COSMO-iso, WRF-iso or LMDZiso been evaluated against station
  observations, and which metrics, resolutions and nudging set-ups were used?
- Where do isotope-enabled models typically show biases in monsoon rainfall δ18O, and what explanations are
  proposed (convection scheme, re-evaporation, resolution)?
- What would a defensible evaluation of IsoRSM against 2024–2026 Port Blair rainfall isotopes require?

## Satellites & validation
- How should averaging kernels and a priori profiles be applied when comparing model vapour δD with IASI, TES or
  MUSICA retrievals?
- What collocation criteria and quality filters were used in satellite–in-situ isotope comparisons?

## Plotting & statistics
- Which plots best show whether a model reproduces event-scale δ18O variability, and what statistics and
  uncertainty estimates should accompany them?
- How should amount-weighted means be computed and compared with model monthly output?
- How can autocorrelation be handled when testing lagged relationships between rainfall and isotopes?

## Research gaps (always verify against the wider literature)
- Which combinations of observations (rainfall, vapour, satellite) and models are missing in my library for the
  Andaman–Bay of Bengal region?
- Which limitations do authors repeatedly state that my 2024–2026 dataset could address?
